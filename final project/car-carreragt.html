Porsche Carrera GT

The Porsche Carrera GT (Type 980) is a 2003-2006 mid-engine supercar powered by a 5.7-liter naturally aspirated V10 engine, producing 612 PS (450 kW) and 590 Nm of torque. Famed for its 6-speed manual transmission and analog driving experience, it accelerates from 0-100 km/h in 3.9 seconds and exceeds 330 km/h, with only 1,270 units produced.
