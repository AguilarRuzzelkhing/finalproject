Toyota AE86

The Toyota Sprinter Trueno AE86 (1983-1987) is a lightweight, rear-wheel-drive sport compact, famous for its pop-up headlights and iconic status in drifting and the Initial D anime. Featuring a 1.6L 4A-GE engine, it is renowned for its handling and balance, often nicknamed "hachi-roku" (Japanese for "eight- six").
