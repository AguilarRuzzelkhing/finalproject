Lamborghini Countach

The Lamborghini Countach is a legendary rear mid- engine, rear-wheel-drive supercar produced from 1974-1990, defining the "Italian Wedge" design with its sharp angles and scissor doors. Designed by Marcello Gandini, it featured V12 engines ranging from 3.9L to 5.2L, evolving over 16 years into an iconic, raw driving machine before being succeeded by the Diablo.
