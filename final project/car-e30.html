BMW E30

The BMW E30 is the second generation of the 3 Series, produced from 1982 to 1994, renowned for its iconic boxy styling, engaging rear-wheel-drive handling, and motorsport success. Available as a sedan, coupe, convertible, and "Touring" wagon, it featured 4 and 6-cylinder engines. It is widely considered a highly collectible, durable classic, with the high-performance E30 M3 being the most famous variant.
