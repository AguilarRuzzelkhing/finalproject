Corvette ZR-1

The 1990 Corvette ZR-1, nicknamed "King of the Hill," debuted in 1989 with a 375-horsepower 5.7L LT5 V8 engine designed with Lotus and built by Mercury Marine. It boasted 32 valves, double overhead cams, a 6-speed manual transmission, and widened rear bodywork for massive tires. Only 3,000 were built in the first year, capable of 0--60 mph in 4.5 seconds.
