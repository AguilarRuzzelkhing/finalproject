Corvette C7

The Chevrolet Corvette (C7) is the seventh generation of American sports cars produced from 2014 to 2019, notable as the final front-engine Corvette. It features a 6.2L LT1 V8 engine producing 455-460 horsepower (base/Z51), paired with a 7-speed manual or 8-speed automatic (2015+) transmission. Used models, especially Z51, are favored for performance-per-dollar, usually found between $40k-$55k
