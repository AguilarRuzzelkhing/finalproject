Honda S2000

The Honda S2000 is a legendary front-mid engine, rear-wheel-drive roadster produced from 1999 to 2009 to celebrate Honda's 50th anniversary. Famous for its high-revving naturally aspirated 2.0L (AP1) or 2.2L (AP2) VTEC engine, it produces roughly 240 horsepower and screams to a 9,000 RPM redline, offering a pure, analog driving experience.
