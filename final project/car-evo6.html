Mitsubishi Evolution VI

The 1999-2001 Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution VI is an iconic JDM sports sedan known for its rally-bred 4G63 2.0L turbocharged engine, 276 hp (280 PS) output, and advanced AWD system. It improved on the Evo V with enhanced cooling, stronger pistons, and a distinctive front bumper with offset fog lights. Key variants included the luxurious GSR and the lightweight RS, notably including the coveted Tommi Mäkinen Edition (TME).
