Ferrari F40

The Ferrari F40, produced from 1987 to 1992, is a legendary mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive supercar created to celebrate Ferrari's 40th anniversary. As the last car personally approved by Enzo Ferrari, it is revered for its raw, unfiltered driving experience, featuring a 471 hp (later 478 hp) 2.9L twin-turbo V8, carbon-fiber construction, and a top speed exceeding 200 mph (324 km/h).
