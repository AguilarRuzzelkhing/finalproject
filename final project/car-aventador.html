Lamborghini Aventador

The Lamborghini Aventador is a iconic mid-engine V12 supercar produced from 2011 to 2022, serving as the brand's flagship. Known for its 6.5L naturally aspirated V12 engine and 7-speed single-clutch transmission, it produces 690 to 780 horsepower, achieving 0-62 mph in under 2.9 seconds with a 217 mph top speed.
