Porsche 917

The Porsche 917 is a legendary sports prototype race car that gave Porsche its first overall wins at the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1970 and 1971. Introduced in 1969 with a 4.5 to 5.0-liter flat-12 engine, it dominated world sportscar racing by 1970 after overcoming early stability issues. The iconic car was renowned for its immense power, lightweight aluminum tubular frame, and high speeds, often reaching nearly 240 mph in long-tail (LH) configuration
