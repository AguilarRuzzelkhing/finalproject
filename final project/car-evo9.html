Mitsubishi Evolution IX

The Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX (2006-2007) is a rally-bred sedan renowned for its 2.0L turbocharged 4G63 engine, now featuring MIVEC technology for improved torque and response. Known as a "supercar slayer," it is considered one of the final "old-school" Evos, producing roughly 276 hp (280 PS) and 353 N-m of torque.
