Civic EK9 Hatchback

The Honda Civic Type R (EK9) is a legendary Japanese Domestic Market (JDM) hot hatch produced between 1997 and 2000. It features a high- revving 1.6L B16B engine producing 182 hp (114 hp/liter), a seam-welded chassis for rigidity, and a helical limited-slip differential. It is renowned for its lightweight, analog driving experience and iconic red Recaro interior.
