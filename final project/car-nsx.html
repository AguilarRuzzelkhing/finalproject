Honda NSX

The Honda NSX Type R (NA1), launched in 1992, is a track-focused, lightweight version of the iconic first-gen NSX, produced exclusively for the Japanese market. It removed nearly 120 kg (265 lbs) of weight-deleting air conditioning, stereo, and sound deadening-and featured stiffer suspension, a 3.0L V6, and a signature Champoinship White finish, making it a highly prized, rare dricer's car.
