Corvette C6

The C6 Chevrolet Corvette (2005-2013) is the sixth generation of the American sports car, notable for returning to exposed headlights and offering a refined, powerful platform with LS-series V8 engines. It is widely considered a high-value used performance car, with models ranging from the 400-hp LS2/LS3 base to the 638-hp ZR1 supercharged beast.
