Toyota Supra MK4

The Toyota Supra MK4 (A80), produced between 1993 and 2002, is a legendary Japanese sports car featuring the highly durable 3.0L twin-turbo 2JZ-GTE engine. Famed for its tuning potential, it became a cultural icon via the Fast and Furious franchise, boasting ~320 hp, a 0-60 mph time of 4.6 seconds, and a robust, rear-wheel-drive platform.
