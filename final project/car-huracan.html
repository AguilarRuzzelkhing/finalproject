Lamborghini Huracán

The Lamborghini Huracán (2014-2024) is a mid- engine, V10-powered sports car succeeding the Gallardo, known for its sharp, hexagonal styling and high-performance capabilities. It features a 5.2L naturally aspirated V10 engine, typically producing over 600hp and 7-speed dual-clutch transmission. Key models include the STO, Tecnica, and Sterrato, with top speeds exceeding 325 km/h.
