Nissan Skyline GT-R R34

The Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 (1999-2002) is an iconic Japanese performance car powered by a 2.6L RB26DETT twin-turbo inline-six engine and the ATTESA E-TS all-wheel-drive system. Known for its advanced technology, including a 5.8-inch multi- function display, it produced a rated 276 hp (often higher in reality) and featured a six-speed Getrag gearbox.
