Mazda 787B

The Mazda 787B is an iconic Group C sports prototype racing car best known for winning the 1991 24 Hours of Le Mans, making it the first Japanese manufacturer and only rotary-powered car to ever win the race. Powered by a high-revving, 700-horsepower R26B 4-rotor engine, it is famous for its distinctive sound and green/orange "Renown" livery
