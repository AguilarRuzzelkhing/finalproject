Mazda Miata

The Mazda Miata NA, produced from 1989 to 1997, is the first-generation Mazda MX-5, widely celebrated for its lightweight, rear-wheel-drive design, iconic pop-up headlights, and engaging driving dynamics. It is a 2- seat, convertible sports car powered by a small four- cylinder engine (1.6L or 1.8L) designed for agility rather than raw power.
