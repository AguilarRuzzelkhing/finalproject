Celica GT4

The Toyota Celica GT-Four is a high-performance, turbocharged all-wheel-drive sports car produced between 1986 and 1999 to homologate Toyota's World Rally Championship (WRC) entries. Known for its 3S-GTE engine and rugged drivetrain, it was released in three generations: ST165, ST185, and ST205. It is a coveted 90s JDM icon, particularly the ST205, known for its WRC, Group A, and rally heritage.
