BMD M4 Competition

The BMW M4 Competition is a high-performance sports coupe (G82) featuring a 3.0L twin-turbo inline- six engine (S58), delivering up to 530 hp and 650 Nm of torque. Known for its aggressive styling, rapid 0- 100 km/h acceleration (3.5s or faster), and available xDrive all-wheel drive, it acts as a track-ready daily driver with sharp, precise handling.
