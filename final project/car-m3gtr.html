BMW M3 GTR

The BMW M3 GTR (E46) is a legendary, limited- production sports car featuring a 4.0L V8 engine ( P60 B40), famously dominating the 2001 American Le Mans Series (ALMS). While the race car produced over 450 hp, a limited number of street-legal "Strassenversion" models were built to satisfy homologation rules, making it famous for its rarity and iconic appearance in Need for Speed: Most Wanted
