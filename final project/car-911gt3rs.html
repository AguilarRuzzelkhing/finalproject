Porsche 911 GT3 RS

The 2025/2026 Porsche 911 GT3 RS (992) is a street- legal, track-focused sports car featuring a 4.0-liter naturally aspirated flat-six engine producing 525 PS (386 kW). It utilizes radical active aerodynamics, including a large swan-neck rear wing with DRS, to generate massive downforce, achieving 0--100 km/h in 3.2 seconds.
