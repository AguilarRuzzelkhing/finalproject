Nissan Silvia S15

he Nissan Silvia S15 (1999-2002) is the final, most refined generation of Nissan's S-platform, renowned as a JDM icon for drifting and tuning. Available primarily as a 2-door coupe, it featured the 2.0L SR20DET turbo engine (Spec-R, $\sim$250 PS) or SR20DE (Spec-S), known for its lightweight, balanced rear-wheel-drive handling, aggressive styling, and 6- speed manual transmission.
