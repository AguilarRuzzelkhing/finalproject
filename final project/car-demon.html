Dodge Demon

The 2023 Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170 is a 1,025-horsepower, limited-production drag-focused muscle car, representing the final "Last Call" model of the Hemi-powered Challenger. It achieves 0-60 mph in 1.66 seconds, with the supercharged 6.2L V8 producing peak power on E85 ethanol. Only 3,300 units were produced, with prices often over $100,000.
