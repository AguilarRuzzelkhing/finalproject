Ferrari F50

The Ferrari F50 (1995-1997) is a, limited-production, Formula 1-derived supercar designed to celebrate Ferrari's 50th anniversary. It features a naturally aspirated 4.7-liter V12 engine producing 513 hp ( 382 kW), mated to a 6-speed manual transmission. Known for its analog, raw driving experience with no power steering or driver aids, only 349 units were built.
